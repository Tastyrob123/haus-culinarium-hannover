The team.

The people behind Culinarium and Walk on the Wild Side.

Three people,one house.

Who's behind the bar, in the kitchen and at the table on an evening.

Runs the bar

Derya Ivkin

Has run Walk on the Wild Side, the home of Culinarium, since the reopening in November 2023. There's no menu with her: come to the bar and you get a drink to your taste.

“I am the menu.”

Derya in the Neue Presse, January 2024
Roots
At home in Hannover, with roots in Ordu on Turkey's Black Sea coast.
2023
She takes over the bar in Linden and reopens it in November, without a drinks menu: you say what you like and get a drink of your own. Momo is how she came to drinks.
The House
Under her the bar becomes a home for more: Culinarium at the long table, bartenders from other bars as guests behind the counter, concerts on Thursdays.
Saturdays
On some Saturdays DJs play, with drinks and music until 02:00.
@derrisch__

Mixes the drinks

Momo

Mohamed Ben Khelifa

Stands behind the bar and mixes the signature drinks, including the five of a Culinarium evening, and explains each one himself.

“Tell me what you like, and I'll make you a drink.”

Momo's motto at the bar
2018
Head bartender at Lindenblatt in Hannover. Even then he mixes drinks of his own, with melon, pink pepper and honey, say.
2023
In May he does the first pop-up at Walk on the Wild Side; in September the first evenings with a menu and his drinks follow. That's how Culinarium begins.
In the glass
He works with mixology techniques: milk makes a drink clear and soft, fat lends the spirit its flavour.
On the road
He's behind a bar outside the bar too, in the summers of 2024 and 2025 at the Maschseefest by the lake, for instance.
@momo012027

Cooks the courses

Rudi Napieraj

Rudolf Napieraj

Has cooked for Culinarium since 2024, three courses at the long table, and runs the kitchen at the Dirty Chicken pop-up.

“A project close to our hearts.”

Rudi on Culinarium, July 2024
2024
In April he cooks his first Culinarium evening. Since then every poster carries two names: Momo for the drinks, Rudi for the kitchen.
On the plate
Vegetables are often at the centre of his plates: leek, cauliflower, pumpkin, beetroot. Every course comes with a matching drink by Momo.
2025
In July he cooks for the first Tacos & Tequila party at the bar, together with Momo.
2026
A menu of his own at the bar for Valentine's Day. He also launches Dirty Chicken, a pop-up with chicken sandwiches, fries and drinks.
@rudi__san
