Your drink,to your taste.

There's no drinks menu at Walk on the Wild Side. You come to the bar, say what you like, and get a drink of your own. Since the reopening on 9 November 2023, Derya Ivkin has run the bar, with Momo beside her behind the counter.

Fatwashing
The spirit takes on the flavour of a fat; chilled, the fat is lifted off again.
Milkwashing
Milk binds the cloudiness and is filtered out; the drink turns clear and soft.
Enzymes
Enzymes clear the haze from fruit juice; the flavour stays.

Every drinkone of a kind.
