Tasting and a short masterclass

First, then, the glass.

The first glass.

Momo mixes the drinks behind the house bar. A cocktail tasting and a short masterclass are part of the evening.

What fat, milk and enzymes do to a drink

Fatwashing

The spirit takes on the flavour of a fat; chilled, the fat is lifted off again.

Milkwashing

Milk binds the cloudiness and is filtered out; the drink turns clear and soft.

Enzymes

Enzymes clear the haze from fruit juice; the flavour stays.
